H4 is material, chiefly flooring and pause inclusion. Exploratory all-ten ZS*: 26.9699% DER -> 24.9067% with floor snapping after the existing same-speaker gap5 decoder. Main 37-rule conditional inner 1-SE selection: ZS 24.9713%, FT 26.7422%; no demonstrated FT advantage. FT frozen 27.6875% -> 27.2724% with floor both, or 26.6845% with offset-only floor. Recommend full-resolution speech intervals without multi-second gap fill as the practical default, and explicit Bengali-Loop mode with base/s20/avg/a.40/c.60, same-speaker silent gaps <=5 s, then floor both endpoints/drop zero-bin turns/merge touching identical IDs.

Evidence: all 2,612 raw rows have integer endpoints; 2,497 valid rows; 109 zero lengths, five reversed, one missing identity. Minimum positive stored duration is 1 s, but 484 one-second rows and 109 zero lengths argue against a hard acoustic minimum. Both cached detectors are silent during 6,941.24 s (25.01%) of reference speech union, including 5,737.16 s at least one second inside turns. There are many real reference gaps: 1,927 positive adjacent-row gaps, 1,324 exactly 1 s; 135 same-speaker positive gaps. Nominal first-speaker overlap policy has 27 cross-speaker overlap pairs (19 nested), leaving 106 excess speaker-seconds in the scoring reference. The overlap defect alone accounts for only a 0.3805% exclusive-system floor.

Do not state that annotators always floored both endpoints. Stable isolated speech-union edges have joint-detector medians +0.27/+0.27 s, but detector choice/filtering sometimes favors rounding and train_004 has negative medians. These are detector proxies; the six supplied WAV/trace examples were not human auditory adjudications. The paper specifies manual HH:MM:SS labels and first-speaker overlap, not the historical quantizer, a minimum duration, or a pause threshold.

Gap >5 s adds little: a disclosed follow-up sweep gives an all-ten minimum at g7+floor of 24.7236%; inner argmin gives 24.8133% and inner 1-SE 24.9713%. Thus the conditional gain over fixed g5+floor is only 0.0933 pp for argmin, and negative for conservative selection. g10 adds 480.87 s over g5, only 272.02 s reference-labelled, so misses saved are cancelled by false alarms/confusion. Short-turn deletion, different-speaker gap extension, A-B-A merging, and earliest-active-onset overlap ownership add no material robust gain. Earliest onset actually worsens fixed ZS floor from 24.9067% to 24.9821%.

FT has learned continuity and onset bias: at common p>=.4 it activates 90.75% of detector-silent turn interiors versus 8.89% for ZS. Raw predicted onset-minus-label medians are -0.01 s FT and +0.25 s ZS; offset medians remain +0.41/+0.36 s. In a matched existing-model F1-inner eight-recording comparison, raw-target FT with offset-floor is 26.4502%, versus 26.3938% ZS gap5/floor; shifted-onset FT is worse (28.9157% with offset-floor). This is an inner diagnostic, not an independent validation or a new fine-tune. Current selected targets shift starts +.44..+.46 s in four folds and exclude long-pause turns; target choice should be reselected jointly with decoding. Exact absolute-second snapping belongs in the decoder because arbitrary sampled and spliced training offsets remove the original clock phase.

Dependence: all results condition on frozen upstream linking and existing caches; better linking may change gap-fill eligibility. No dependence on another council role, GPU work, new model training, repository modifications, or submission. Scoring was verified with 180 real + 36 synthetic pyannote comparisons and 272 total assertions. All-ten results and confidence intervals are exploratory and conditional on reused data. VAD agreement is not acoustic gold, but ZS activity F1 versus independent Silero falls 90.92 -> 81.80 -> 78.70% from ungapped to g5 to g5/floor, so benchmark gain must not be advertised as improved physical timing.

Open questions are exact annotator timing-tool semantics, the official scorer's overlap/collar handling, independent fine-resolution Bengali labels, and a future equal-budget raw-target FT versus decoded-base evaluation.
